Typography

Four typefaces organized into two pairs: Roboto ↔ Noto Sans KR for interface use and Englebert ↔ Poor Story for visual accents.

Primary pair · interface

Roboto is the Latin base. Noto Sans KR is its Korean counterpart, preserving readability, weights, and structure across both languages.

Display pair · identity

Englebert gives Diarito its more playful gesture. Poor Story fills that role in Korean and works well for short titles, stickers, and identity moments.
