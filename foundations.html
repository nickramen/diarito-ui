Colors & tokens

Diarito color system. Blue leads; all brand roles and states are aliases of real base colors.

1. Brand

The five brand roles do not create new colors: they point directly to Blue, Green, Pink, Purple, and Gray.

PrimaryBlue 500
--diarito-primary-500
SecondaryGreen 500
--diarito-secondary-500
Accent 1Pink 500
--diarito-accent-1-500
Accent 2Purple 500
--diarito-accent-2-500
NeutralGray 500
--diarito-neutral-500

2. Usage

When one family builds a contextual surface, 300 is the background and 700 is the text/icon color. 500 remains the main fill.

Primary / BlueBlue 300 background · Blue 700 text
Secondary / GreenGreen 300 background · Green 700 text
Accent 1 / PinkPink 300 background · Pink 700 text
Accent 2 / PurplePurple 300 background · Purple 700 text
Neutral / GrayGray 300 background · Gray 700 text

3. States

The four semantic states reuse existing families. There is no hidden second palette for feedback.

SuccessSuccess = Green · 300 background + 700 text
InfoInfo = Blue · 300 background + 700 text
WarningWarning = Yellow · 300 background + 700 text
DangerDanger = Red · 300 background + 700 text
